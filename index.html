About

One day, six ways to win

STEM FEST is a competition, and it is meant to be a laugh. Students from every participating school in one building, going at the same problems at the same time. Olympiads in the morning, project tables buzzing all day, and an FC 26 bracket running in the corner with a crowd around it. Come for one event, come for several, come with your whole class. Bragging rights are the real prize.

Can I enter more than one event?

Yes. Pick as many as you like on the registration form, as long as the schedules do not clash. The form will show you what your class is eligible for.

My school is not listed.

Pick "Other" on the registration form and type your school name in. Everything else works exactly the same way.

I am in class 7. Can I enter the class 9 olympiad?

No. Each olympiad is written for a class band, and you will only be shown events your class is eligible for. This keeps the marking fair.

Is the gaming tournament separate?

It runs on the same day and is open to every age group, so all classes share one bracket.

What do I need to bring?

Your school ID card and your own instruments. Project entrants should bring everything their build needs, including power adaptors.

Where do I sign up?

Everything happens on the Register tab. Pick your class and school and the form guides you from there.
