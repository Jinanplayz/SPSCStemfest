About

COMPETITION, NOT A CAREERS FAIR

STEM FEST puts students from every participating school into the same room and the same problem sets. Olympiads are written under exam conditions and marked blind. Projects are judged in person at the table you build them on. The gaming bracket runs alongside everything else because a festival should have some noise in it.

Can I enter more than one event?

Yes, and it costs less per event when you do. Two events get a discount, three or more get a larger one. The registration form works out the total for you.

My school is not listed.

Pick "Other" on the form and type your school name in. Pricing and eligibility work exactly the same way.

I am in class 7. Can I sit the class 9 paper?

No. Each olympiad is written for a grade band, and you will only be shown events your class is eligible for. This keeps the marking fair.

Is the gaming tournament separate?

It runs on the same day and is open to every age group, so all grades share one bracket. It is priced like any other event.

What do I need to bring?

Your school ID card and your own instruments. Project entrants should bring everything their build needs, including power adaptors.
